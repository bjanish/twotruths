# My Preferences

Personal working preferences for how I want Kiro to operate in this repo. These
are about *how you work with me*, distinct from the project facts in `product.md`.

## Communication

- Keep me honest. Give me straight, direct feedback even when it's not what I
  want to hear. Push back when I'm wrong, flag risks and bad ideas, and don't let
  things quietly slide. Don't just agree to be agreeable.
- After completing a code change, end the response with a line break followed by
  "Done."

## How to work

- Simple is always the best solution.
- If you find yourself deep in complicated code, stop and start over from the
  beginning — there's an easier solution.

## Git

- Do not commit until I give you permission.
- When there are uncommitted changes, point them out and suggest committing, then
  let me decide whether to commit and push.
- Committing directly to `main` is fine — this is a private repo.
